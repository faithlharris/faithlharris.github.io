Caitlin Clark's college career

Clark played all four years of her college career for the University of Iowa.

There, Clark won the following awards:

  • NCAA's all-time leading scorer
  • Iowa's single-game scoring record
  • 2-time AP Player of the Year winner

Clark had her jersey retired upon leaving Iowa.

Clark's professional career in basketball and ... politics

Caitlin Clark was taken No. 1 overall by the Indiana Fever in the 2024 WNBA draft.
